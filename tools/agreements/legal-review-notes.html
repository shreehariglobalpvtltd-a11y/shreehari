5. Points added in v2.1 that need legal review · कानुनी जाँच चाहिने थप दफा

These come from the owner's own drafts ("shari agrement for pvt.pdf", "rule for agg.pdf"), not from the 26-09 contract. They are in all four languages. Ask a lawyer before the first signing.

ClausePointWhy a lawyer should look
2.3Tax cut as the law requires cannot be claimed from the Company later.A party cannot waive a right the tax law gives it (for example a TDS credit).
2.4No commission / pay on a ticket made outside the Company's system or approved ticket books.Check it is not read as forfeiting pay already earned.
6.2No diverting passengers, customers, agents or staff; for ____ years after the end, no diverting customers (secrecy stays open-ended, as 6.1).After-term restraints are narrowly enforced in India (Contract Act s.27). Keep the years short and the scope to the Company's own customers.
6.3Everything done from the agent's login ID is the agent's responsibility.Consider an exception for misuse the agent reported at once.
7.1The loss the records prove includes harm to the name, lost customers and legal costs.Only a loss caused by the rule broken, and reasonable in amount, is recoverable (s.73).
7.2Money owed may be cut from commission, advance or deposit, and held back until paid, after the written notice.Set-off and holding back must be in proportion to the proved dues.
7.3The extra 10% (unchanged: tick box, only after legal advice).See section 2.
7.4Holding back passenger money "will be treated as" embezzlement; complaints to police and courts in Nepal or India.A contract cannot make something a crime; keep it as a statement of the Company's right to complain.
7.5 (partner)The partner's director or owner takes on every duty personally.A director of a Pvt. Ltd. is not liable for the company unless he signs a separate personal guarantee. Have the director sign as a guarantor.
7.6 / 7.5, 11.3One or two guarantors in Nepal and one or two in India, jointly liable; their declaration and table on the last page.A guarantee is a separate contract (s.126): each guarantor must sign, and a Nepal guarantor may need a Nepali-law form.
8.4The Company may close the login ID and end the agreement at once for money not handed over, fraud, diverting customers or brand misuse.Keep a written record of the reason, as 7.2 asks for deductions.
10.2No responsibility when service stops for a bandh, strike, disaster, road block or government ban.Check it does not also excuse refunds owed to passengers.
10.3System records, logs, emails, SMS and WhatsApp are "valid and final" proof.Courts decide what proof is final; electronic records need the Evidence Act certificate. "Final" may be read down.
11.2Giving a false document "is a separate offence".Same as 7.4: the offence comes from the criminal law, not the contract.
