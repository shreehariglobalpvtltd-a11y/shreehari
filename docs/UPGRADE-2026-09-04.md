# Upgrade — 4 Sep 2026 (plain language)

Serial pass over **Settings → Booking (login) → Pricing → Passenger → Ticket → Map**, each
module checked first and then fixed, tested locally, and deployed to **shreehariglobal.in** /
**shreehariglobal.network** (build `shg-v87`, assets `?v=20260904b`).

---

## 1. Settings

- **Bug fixed:** every press of *Save all settings* was wrapping the JSON settings
  (`cabin_pricing`, `refund_slabs`, `loyalty_tiers`, `travel_checklist`) in one more layer of
  escaping. On live they had become unreadable and the app was silently using code defaults.
  The writer now decodes first, and a typo in a JSON box is refused with a message instead of
  being saved. The four live rows were repaired in place (5 layers peeled).
- Because the row became readable again, the **private cabin prices** it held (old ₹3,600 /
  ₹7,000) were re-set to the agreed **₹3,800 / ₹7,600**, direction fares inside it corrected to
  Gujarat→Rupaidiha ₹2,000 / Rupaidiha→Gujarat ₹1,800, and online = offline.

## 2. Booking — sign-in is **name + mobile**, nothing else

- The *Sign in* button opens two boxes: **Full name** and **Mobile number**. No OTP, no
  password. A new number is registered on the spot; a returning one is welcomed back.
- The **checkout signs the traveller in by itself** from the same two boxes, so the booking is
  tied to their account and the ticket carries exactly those details.
- **My Bookings** now lists every ticket the account holds, including counter sales made on
  the same number, from the server (not just this phone's memory).
- The lead passenger's name is required (it is the sign-in name and prints on the ticket);
  other passengers in a group stay optional as before.
- OTP still exists for cancellations and as a fallback. Suspended accounts are refused.

## 3. Pricing — fare by passenger count

- Seat map, checkout and the ticket all show a line like **"2 passengers × ₹2,000 = ₹4,000"**
  (in Nepali/Hindi too), so a family sees how the total was built.
- The **"How many seats?"** buttons follow *Admin → Settings → max seats per booking*.

## 4. Passenger / 5. Ticket

- Ticket page shows **Booked by: name · +91 number** (the sign-in details).
- PDF and image tickets print "N × ₹fare" in the passenger line for a group.

## 6. Map

- Map is fenced to **India + Nepal** and cannot be dragged off to the ocean.
- The Trip Companion map now draws the run from the **database stops** (same rows as the
  timetable) and highlights **your own pickup and drop** in orange with a label.
- Speed display recolours by pace: walking / cycle / car / bus.
- Four map providers (geocoder, nearby places, Satellite and Terrain styles) were being
  blocked by the security policy on live; they now work.
- Seat map: every seat state has an icon as well as a colour — ✓ selected, ✕ booked,
  ⏳ held by someone else, ♀/♂ women-/men-only cabin, 🔒 private cabin — and the legend
  matches what is drawn.

## Look & feel / phones

- Dark mode paints black from the very first frame (no white flash), and the phone's status
  bar follows the theme.
- Touch targets raised to 48 px on the seat-count buttons, checkout Back, saved-traveller
  chips; modal close 44 px.
- Short vibration on seat pick / booking sent / refusal (Android).
- Network calls give up after 25 s with a clear message instead of spinning forever.
- First-visit splash floor cut from 6.5 s to 2.5 s.

## Security

- OTP endpoint capped per IP (10 codes / 5 min) — stops SMS-pumping.
- One-time codes are no longer stored readable in the message log.
- Bookings from a signed-in customer are written against **their** number only.
- Agent sign-up form carries a CSRF token; rate limiter is now atomic.

## Server

- nginx: versioned assets cached 1 year + immutable, unversioned 7 days; brotli enabled;
  JS/font MIME types added to compression. PHP-FPM workers 5 → 24. MariaDB buffer pool
  128 MB → 1 GB.

## Still open (owner decision)

- A **Dokploy** panel with Postgres runs on the same VPS, reachable from the internet on port
  3000 with the firewall off. Not touched. Remove it or firewall it.

## Tests

# Batch 2 — same day, later (`shg-v88`, assets `?v=20260904c`)

## Seats

- **Both floors on one scroll.** The seat map shows Floor 1 and Floor 2 together, each with a
  sticky label. Chips at the top: **Any / Floor 1 / Floor 2**; the choice is remembered on the
  phone and also steers the automatic pick.
- **Smarter "How many seats?"** — the picker keeps a group in one row, gives a solo traveller a
  window seat, and never picks a women-only seat by default.
- **🩺 Patient / elderly chip** on the seat screen: suggests a lower-deck, aisle-side seat and
  flags the lead passenger for priority boarding on the ticket, the manifest and the booking
  view (new column `booking_passengers.special_need`).
- **Women-only seats are now enforced** for online bookings in sharing / seater mode (a male
  passenger is refused with a clear message; the checkout warns before submit). Counter staff
  can still override. Private cabins are whole cabins, so the rule does not apply there.
- **Holds cannot overlap across Sharing ⇄ Private any more.** A private cabin hold now blocks
  the two sharing beds under it and vice-versa (holds are stored as physical beds; a cabin is
  two rows acquired in order with rollback). Switching Sharing ⇄ Private releases the old
  holds. Admin "release hold" frees the whole cabin. All live holds were reset once at deploy.
- The seat poll in Private mode compares the right seat names now; a killed tab restores the
  T&C tick; an active checkout keeps its seats alive every 8 minutes.

## Map

- **Real cross-device bus position.** A driver signed in as staff who taps "I'm the driver"
  on the navigator publishes the GPS fix every 10 s to the shared store. Passengers' Trip
  Companion shows "Bus is at … 🛰️ · 42 km/h" and moves the 🚌 marker on the trip map (polls
  every 12 s while live). Any staff role may publish; the office's "Set position" still works.
- Dead Firebase scripts no longer requested on trip pages.

## Small things

- Service worker no longer downloads the app twice on first install.
- AI helper capped at 8 calls / 5 min and 60 / day per IP; agent commission rules no longer
  readable by the public; landscape safe areas; install-banner close button 44 px.
- Test suites: `cross-mode-hold` 32 · `women-only` 7 · all earlier suites green.

## Server

- Dokploy panel scaled to zero (port 3000 closed; revert: `docker service scale dokploy=1
  dokploy-postgres=1`). MariaDB restarted: InnoDB buffer pool is now 1 GB.

  switch it back **ON**. Per-date cancel / block stays on the Schedule page.

# Batch 4 — 5 Sep 2026 (`shg-v90`, assets `?v=20260904e`)

## Admin panel — Customers & Agents registers

- **Admin → Customers** is now an Excel-style register: every customer the company knows
  (accounts from name + mobile sign-in **and** every number that ever booked, even before
  sign-in existed). Columns: name, mobile, document, bookings, seats, spent, last trip, last
  booking (PNR + status), status. One page, scrolls; search box, status / bookings / month
  filters, click any header to sort. **View** expands the row with all details; **Edit**
  (name, country, email, document); **Block / Unblock**; **Delete** only when the person has no
  bookings (super-admin); **History** and **PDF** links kept; **Export CSV / Excel**.
- **Admin → Agents List** (new, People section): name, mobile, SHG code, city / counter,
  sales, revenue, commission due, cash held, status (active / pending / deactivated), last
  sale. Same search / filter / sort / View / Edit / Activate-Deactivate / Delete (only with no
  sales or wallet history) / Export CSV / Excel. Code, PIN, password and route permissions
  stay on Staff & Agents; the row links there and to the agent's sales / passengers / wallet.
- Both tables scroll sideways on a phone with the name column pinned; nothing changes for
  the booking flow.

## Route overview card (animated)

- Under the home search card and at the top of every ticket: a dark map card with the real
  India and Nepal outlines, the traveller's own boarding town and destination placed by
  their coordinates, a dashed arc with the distance and hours from the route data, a bus
  riding the arc, pulsing end points and a gentle 3D tilt / float. Follows the chosen
  direction and town on the home page. Static under "reduce motion". It is a route
  overview, not the live position (the trip page has that).
