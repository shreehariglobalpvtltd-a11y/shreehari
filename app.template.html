The route

To the border, closer to home

From Surat, Vadodara and Ahmedabad to Rupaidiha — the India-side border stop for Nepalgunj.

India ⇄ NepalRupaidiha is in India, at the border. Nepalgunj is just across, in Nepal.

Illustrative corridor — not a road map, distance or live GPS position.

Illustrative map: a route line from Gujarat to Rupaidiha on the India–Nepal border
  1. दिशा छान्नुहोस्माथिको बुकिङ बाकसमा “नेपाल जाने” (गुजरात → रुपैडिहा) वा “गुजरात फर्कने” (रुपैडिहा → गुजरात) थिच्नुहोस्।
  2. यात्राको मितिमिति पट्टीमा आफ्नो दिन थिच्नुहोस्। धेरै पछिको मिति चाहिए “यात्रा मिति” बाकसको क्यालेन्डर प्रयोग गर्नुहोस्।
  3. चढ्ने / ओर्लने ठाउँआफूलाई नजिक पर्ने सहर र समय छान्नुहोस् — जस्तै Surat, Vadodara, Ahmedabad (Chiloda)। यात्री संख्या मिलाउनुहोस्।
  4. बस र सिट हेर्नुहोस्बटन थिचेपछि बस र खाली बर्थ देखिन्छ। सिट नक्सामा खाली बर्थमा थिच्नुहोस् — जति यात्री, त्यति बर्थ।
  5. यात्रीको विवरणहरेक यात्रीको नाम र एउटा मोबाइल नम्बर लेख्नुहोस्। WhatsApp चल्ने नम्बर दिनुहोस् — टिकट त्यहीँ आउँछ।
  6. भुक्तानीस्क्रिनमा देखिएको तरिका छान्नुहोस् — UPI / eSewa बाट तिर्नुहोस् वा सुविधा भएमा बस चढ्दा नगद। तिरेको स्क्रिनसट माग्यो भने अपलोड गर्नुहोस्।
  7. टिकट पाउनुहोस्टिकट पक्का भएपछि स्क्रिनमा र WhatsApp मा आउँछ। “मेरो टिकट” मा जहिले पनि फेरि हेर्न र डाउनलोड गर्न सकिन्छ। बस चढ्दा टिकट (PNR) देखाउनुहोस्।

About the route

A friendship you can board

India and Nepal share one of the world's few open borders. Under the 1950 Treaty of Peace and Friendship, citizens of both nations may cross freely — no visa, no passport stamp — carrying only a valid identity document.

Our coach turns that friendship into a single, comfortable journey: from your boarding point across Gujarat, up through North India, to the Rupaidiha ⇄ Jamunaha border — the gateway onward to Nepalgunj and Nepal's Banke district.

The same road carries families, pilgrims, students and workers. Two flags, displayed side by side with respect; one road, travelled together.

Frequently asked questions

Indian and Nepali citizens do not need a visa for each other's country, but every passenger must carry a valid government photo ID (such as a passport or voter ID). Documents are checked at boarding and may be checked at the Rupaidiha ⇄ Jamunaha border. Requirements can change — please confirm current rules before travelling.

The road distance is roughly 1,300 km and the coach takes about 30 hours including meal halts and the border crossing. All times on this site and on your ticket are India time (IST); Nepal (NPT) is 15 minutes ahead.

You pay directly to the company's UPI ID shown at checkout, then submit either a payment screenshot or the UPI transaction (UTR) number. Our team manually verifies every payment; your seat is held meanwhile and your e-ticket is released the moment we confirm it.

As soon as your payment is verified (usually within 30 minutes during 9 AM – 9 PM IST), your booking status changes to Confirmed and a branded PDF ticket with a boarding QR code becomes available to download.

Yes — cancellations and one-time rescheduling are allowed as per the timelines in our Ticket Booking Terms (for example, higher refunds the earlier you cancel; no refund for no-shows). See the Terms & Conditions page for the exact policy.

Each passenger may carry personal luggage. All luggage must comply with Indian and Nepali customs rules — prohibited or commercial goods cannot be carried as personal baggage. For commercial cargo, use our import–export service instead.
