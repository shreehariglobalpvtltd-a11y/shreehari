PART B — TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT

Both parties agree to the following terms:

■ 1. Parties, purpose and authorized work

1.1The Company carries on a travel business (passenger bus transport and ticket booking).
1.2The Company appoints the Partner, acting through its authorized staff, to bring passengers and to make their online/offline ticket bookings in the service area stated in Part A.
1.3The Partner shall do only the work authorized by the Company, and shall make every booking only at Company-approved fares, through the Company’s system and procedures.
1.4The Partner is responsible for the work of every person who acts on its behalf under this Agreement.

■ 2. Booking targets, commission and payment timing

2.1Commission / service fee: ₹ per ticket or % of the fare, as confirmed in writing by the Company; monthly booking target, if any: passengers. No salary or fixed monthly payment applies unless stated here: .
2.2Payment: on the day of the following month, after booking and account reconciliation, by bank transfer / cheque / UPI, after deducting TDS, GST and other taxes as required by applicable law.

■ 3. Customer payments, daily collection handover and reconciliation

3.1The Partner shall record every cash or online amount received from passengers, give the Company a full account every day, and deposit the amount with the Company.
3.2The Partner shall keep proof of every amount received (cash, UPI or bank) and produce it whenever the Company asks.
3.3Bookings and accounts shall be reconciled before each monthly payment; any difference shall be recorded in writing and settled in that payment.

■ 4. Authorized fares, discounts, ticketing, passenger communication and service conduct

4.1The Partner shall use only the fares, discounts, offers, services and facilities approved by the Company, and shall issue tickets only through the Company’s system or approved ticket books.
4.2Passengers shall be given correct information; no false or misleading promotion or promise shall be made. Full responsibility for any service, fare, discount or offer not approved by the Company rests with the Partner, and the Company may take the necessary legal action.
4.3The Partner and its staff shall treat passengers courteously and follow the Company’s service procedures.

■ 5. Company name, logo, ticket design, photographs and promotional material

5.1Neither the Partner nor any person acting on its behalf shall use, copy or misuse the name, logo, brand, photographs, ticket design, identity or promotional material of S HARI GLOBAL PRIVATE LIMITED without the Company’s written permission.
5.2Material approved by the Company may be used only for Company bookings, and shall be withdrawn when the Company asks or when this Agreement ends.

■ 6. Confidentiality

6.1The Partner shall keep the Company’s passenger data, fare lists, agent lists, system logins and other business information confidential, during this Agreement and after it ends, and shall not give them to any other person or competing company.
6.2Login IDs and passwords issued by the Company shall be used only by authorized users and shall not be shared with anyone else.

■ 7. Rule breaches, losses, written notice and deductions

7.1If the Partner (or any person acting on its behalf) breaches the Company’s rules, terms or set procedures, or makes a mistake that causes the Company a loss, the actual loss proved by records may be recovered from, or deducted out of, amounts payable to the Partner.
7.2Before any deduction the Company shall give written notice, and shall keep the reason and the calculation on record.
7.3An additional 10% of the loss amount applies only if the Company, after legal advice, marks “Applies” here: Applies Does not apply.

■ 8. Term, renewal, notice, early termination and serious breach

8.1This Agreement is valid for 11 (eleven) months from the start date stated in Part A.
8.2On expiry, the Company has full discretion to renew this Agreement after reviewing the Partner’s work, conduct, capability and the Company’s needs.
8.3The Partner shall not end this Agreement before its term without reasonable cause and at least 30 (thirty) days’ written notice.
8.4If the Partner leaves without notice, commits fraud, misuses the Company’s name, system or funds, or seriously breaches the rules, the Company may end this Agreement immediately and take the necessary action under its rules and applicable law.

■ 9. Return of Company records, ticket books, login access and outstanding funds

9.1When this Agreement ends, the Partner shall hand over to the Company the complete accounts, outstanding amounts, documents, ticket books, login access and all Company material, and shall give the necessary cooperation.
9.2Amounts due between the parties shall be settled after the final reconciliation.

■ 10. Change in law, dispute discussion and jurisdiction

10.1If a change in government rules, the Companies Act, transport law or any other applicable law makes it impossible to continue this Agreement, both parties may end it after settling mutual dues, outstanding payments and accounts.
10.2Any dispute relating to this Agreement shall first be resolved through mutual discussion. If it is not resolved, this Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Mehsana (Gujarat) shall have jurisdiction.

■ 11. Declaration, signatures and witnesses

11.1This Agreement is acceptable to, valid for and binding on both parties. Both parties have read and understood it and accept it of their own free will, without any pressure. Where the law allows, it also binds their authorized representatives and successors.
