1. Parties

This Agreement is between S Hari Global Pvt Ltd (CIN: U52291GJ2026PTC174029), registered in Mehsana, Gujarat, India ("the Company"), and the individual whose name appears on the approved Agent Registration Form ("the Agent").

2. Appointment

The Company appoints the Agent as a non-exclusive ticket-selling partner for its scheduled cross-border bus service between India and Nepal. The Agent is not an employee; this is a commission-based partnership.

3. Agent Code & Identity

Upon approval, the Agent receives a unique code (e.g. SHG-021) printed on every ticket the Agent sells. The code is personal and non-transferable. The Agent must not share their login credentials or allow anyone else to use their Agent Portal account.

4. Commission

  • Rate: ₹200 per passenger for each confirmed direct booking attributed to the Agent's code.
  • Eligibility: Commission is earned only on bookings that are confirmed, not cancelled, and where the passenger actually travels.
  • Calculation: The Company calculates commission automatically based on the booking system records.
  • No double commission: A booking can earn commission for only one Agent.

5. Wallet & Payouts

  • Earned commissions are credited to the Agent's digital wallet in the Agent Portal.
  • The Agent may request a payout at any time when the wallet balance is ₹1 or above.
  • Payouts are processed to the Agent's registered bank account or UPI within 7 business days.
  • The Company may withhold payout if any commission is under dispute or review.

6. Agent Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information to passengers about routes, schedules and fares.
  • Not charge passengers any amount above the published fare.
  • Not misrepresent the Company's services, brand or policies.
  • Maintain the confidentiality of passenger data encountered through the Agent Portal.
  • Comply with all applicable laws of India and Nepal.

7. Company Responsibilities

  • Provide the Agent with a working Agent Portal, referral link, and QR code.
  • Calculate and credit commissions accurately and on time.
  • Process payout requests within the stated timeframe.
  • Notify the Agent of changes to commission rates, routes or policies with reasonable notice.

8. Prohibited Conduct

  • Fake bookings or self-referral schemes to generate false commission.
  • Collecting money from passengers outside the official booking system.
  • Using the Company's name, logo or materials in any misleading way.
  • Sharing Agent Portal access with unauthorised persons.

9. Suspension & Termination

  • The Company may suspend or revoke the Agent's code if the Agent violates this Agreement.
  • Either party may terminate this Agreement with 15 days' written notice.
  • On termination, outstanding confirmed commissions will be paid; pending or disputed commissions may be withheld pending review.
  • The Agent must stop using the Company's name and materials immediately upon termination.

10. Limitation of Liability

The Company's total liability to the Agent under this Agreement shall not exceed the total commissions earned by the Agent in the preceding 3 months. The Company is not liable for any indirect, consequential or incidental damages.

11. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute shall first be raised through the Agent Portal or by contacting the Company's office. If not resolved within 30 days, disputes shall be settled by arbitration in Mehsana, Gujarat, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

12. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. For agents operating from Nepal, the Nepal-side obligations are governed by Nepali law to the extent applicable.

1. Parties

This Agreement is between S Hari Global Pvt Ltd (CIN: U52291GJ2026PTC174029), registered in Mehsana, Gujarat, India ("the Company"), and the travel agency, counter or business entity whose details appear on the approved Agent Registration Form ("the Organization").

2. Appointment

The Company appoints the Organization as a non-exclusive ticket-selling partner. The Organization may designate a Contact Person to manage the Agent Portal. The Organization is not an employee or franchise; this is a commission-based commercial partnership.

3. Agent Code & Identity

Upon approval, the Organization receives a unique code (e.g. SHG-005) in the 1–20 serial range reserved for organizations. This code appears on every ticket sold through the Organization's account. The code belongs to the Organization; internal staff changes do not require a new code, but the Organization must notify the Company of any change of Contact Person.

4. Commission

  • Rate: ₹200 per passenger for direct bookings; ₹400 for group/team bookings of 5 or more passengers in a single transaction.
  • Eligibility: Commission is earned only on confirmed, completed journeys.
  • Volume incentives: The Company may offer additional bonuses based on monthly booking volume, at its discretion.
  • Invoicing: The Organization may request a monthly commission statement for its accounting records.

5. Wallet & Payouts

  • Earned commissions are credited to the Organization's wallet in the Agent Portal.
  • Payout requests may be submitted at any time when the balance is ₹1 or above.
  • Payouts are processed to the Organization's registered bank account within 7 business days.
  • The Company may request a valid GST invoice where applicable.

6. Organization Responsibilities

  • Ensure all staff who access the Agent Portal are trained on correct booking procedures and passenger data confidentiality.
  • Not charge passengers any amount above the published fare.
  • Not misrepresent the Company's services, schedule or brand.
  • Maintain records of tickets sold and comply with tax obligations (GST/PAN where applicable).
  • Notify the Company immediately of any security breach or unauthorized access to the Agent Portal.
  • Comply with all applicable laws of India and Nepal.

7. Company Responsibilities

  • Provide the Organization with Agent Portal access, referral link, QR code and marketing materials.
  • Calculate and credit commissions accurately.
  • Process payout requests within the stated timeframe.
  • Provide reasonable notice of changes to commission rates, routes or policies.
  • Provide monthly commission statements on request.

8. Prohibited Conduct

  • Fake bookings, self-referral or any scheme to generate false commission.
  • Collecting cash from passengers outside the official booking system.
  • Sub-licensing the Agent Code to third parties without the Company's written consent.
  • Using the Company's name, logo or brand in advertising without prior approval.

9. Suspension & Termination

  • The Company may suspend or terminate this Agreement if the Organization breaches any term.
  • Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice.
  • On termination, confirmed commissions are paid; disputed amounts may be withheld pending review.
  • The Organization must cease using the Company's name, brand and materials upon termination.
  • Termination does not affect bookings already confirmed under the Organization's code.

10. Indemnification

The Organization shall indemnify the Company against any claim, loss or liability arising from the Organization's or its staff's breach of this Agreement, misrepresentation to passengers, or violation of applicable law.

11. Limitation of Liability

The Company's total liability to the Organization under this Agreement shall not exceed the total commissions earned in the preceding 6 months. Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential or incidental damages.

12. Dispute Resolution

Disputes shall first be raised through the Agent Portal or the Company's Mehsana office. If not resolved within 30 days, disputes shall be settled by arbitration in Mehsana, Gujarat, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

13. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. For organizations operating from Nepal, the Nepal-side obligations are governed by Nepali law to the extent applicable.
